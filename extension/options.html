How to find your selectors:
1. Open the alerts page and press F12 to open DevTools.
2. Click the element picker (top-left of DevTools) and click an alert row.
3. Right-click the highlighted element → Copy → Copy selector.
4. Paste it here and simplify if needed (e.g. remove :nth-child parts).

How to find the trigger field internal name:
1. Open the alert page and press F12.
2. Press Ctrl+F in DevTools and search for FieldInternalName.
3. Find the comment next to the field you want (e.g. SagsType) and copy the value inside FieldInternalName="...".
